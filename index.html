    About WellPath

    What this tool is — and what it isn't

    WellPath is an AI-powered student wellness tool built on a machine learning model trained on real student lifestyle data. It considers sleep, physical activity, screen usage, stress levels, and academic habits to generate a personal wellness signal (0–10).

    🤖

    ML-Powered Score

    Trained on real student data to reflect actual patterns.

    📈

    Progress Tracking

    History saves locally — watch your trend over time.

    🧠

    Smart Recommendations

    Personalised to your exact numbers, not generic advice.

    🔒

    Fully Private

    Nothing leaves your device except the prediction call.

    Tech stack: Python (FastAPI + scikit-learn + joblib) backend · Vanilla JS + CSS frontend · Deployed on Render

    ⚠️ Not a clinical diagnosis. If you're experiencing significant distress, please reach out to a mental health professional or someone you trust.

    For informational purposes only — not a clinical assessment. If you're struggling, please talk to someone you trust.
