## ✨ What is WellPath?

**WellPath** helps students understand their wellness patterns through a simple assessment and personalized insights.

###  Key Features

- **Wellness Score** — Get an overall wellness snapshot
- **AI-Powered Insights** — Understand your wellness patterns
- **Personalized Recommendations** — Practical suggestions for improvement
- **Progress Tracking** — View your wellness journey over time
- **Wellness Resources** — Helpful mental-health resources
- **AI Wellness Assistant** — Get quick guidance and support
- **Privacy-Focused** — Designed with student privacy in mind
